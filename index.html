Overnight Developments

watch CoreWeave: credit markets price fresh record distress while the stock rallies on Jensen Huang's defense

CoreWeave's 5yr credit-default swaps printed a record ~1,190bps intraday (implied default odds >60%, up from yesterday's already-distressed ~63% 5yr read) and the 2031 bonds held near 15.8% yield, with bondholders selling throughout the session. The stock told a different story: it fell ~9.5% at Thursday's open (bottoming ~$59.50) before closing +5.94% at ~$69.69, after NVIDIA CEO Jensen Huang said at a Bloomberg event in Stockholm: "CoreWeave's strength is improving every day. I'm optimistic. Their cost of capital is actually decreasing," adding NVIDIA sees no need to backstop the company. Today's premarket gives back part of that equity rally (CRWV -5.29%, NBIS -3.24% in sympathy) even as the credit read stays at its worst level of the cycle. The divergence is itself the signal: equity is trading on a single reassurance from NVIDIA's CEO — simultaneously CoreWeave's largest supplier and a reported investor/counterparty, not a disinterested voice — while credit holders, who are paid only if ~$50B+ of debt is serviced, are pricing the balance sheet on its own terms. Logged Strong (sub-thesis #5) in theses/computational-abundance.md. Still not the specific P008 trigger. (Bloomberg; Benzinga)

bull Samsung/SK Hynix rebound; Kospi snaps 7-session losing streak as analysts call the rout "excessive"

Samsung Electronics (+2.62%) and SK Hynix (+0.79%) rebounded in Seoul as the Kospi ended its longest losing streak of the cycle. The two stocks had fallen 23.9% and 31.5% respectively over seven straight sessions since Oct. 6 (the CXMT-scare-to-CoreWeave-credit-crisis stretch this tracker has logged in real time). Analysts cited in The Korea Times said the sell-off reflected weak buying confidence, not deteriorating fundamentals — memory pricing remains firm (DDR5 spot prices rising), and SK Securities is revising 2027 earnings forecasts upward on an expected deepening of the memory shortage. One caveat worth holding onto: a KB Securities analyst noted the market has shifted toward valuing memory names as commodity cyclicals again, on concern about AI-investment sustainability beyond the next 12 months — the same world-vs-market tension logged repeatedly this cycle, just running in the opposite (recovery) direction today. No prediction impact — P015 (DRAM undersupply through C3Q26) is already at its 95% ceiling and resolves on contract pricing, not equity sentiment. (The Korea Times; KED Global)
