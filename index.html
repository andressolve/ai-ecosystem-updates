Overnight Developments

watch NVIDIA's Megaspeed-linked Singapore revenue disclosure change

NVIDIA reported $10.2B of Singapore-attributed revenue (22% of total, +81% YoY) for the fiscal quarter ended July 2025 — the last period it broke out that geography. The following month, by NVIDIA's own account, it placed Megaspeed International on an internal shipment block list; Megaspeed is the Singapore cloud-computing firm now embroiled in the Alice Huang espionage allegations that preceded Jensen Huang's resignation. WSJ reports the timing but explicitly says it is not established whether the reporting change and the blacklisting are related, and NVIDIA has not explained why it stopped disclosing the geography. This is a disclosure-integrity question, not a confirmed finding — flagging it because it sits upstream of both the 11/17 earnings call and the 11/18 House briefing on Huang, and a reporter or committee member connecting the same dots WSJ did would be a live risk into that week. (WSJ, via Yahoo Finance syndication)
