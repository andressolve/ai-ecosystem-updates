Overnight Developments

bull ByteDance, Alibaba, Tencent order >$35B of China-compliant Nvidia chips; Nvidia can fill only 5-10%

Reuters (exclusive, sourced) reports ByteDance, Alibaba, and Tencent alone have ordered more than 3 million RTX PRO 5500 cards (~$11,400 each, >$35B combined) — Nvidia's export-compliant SKU for China, a deliberately downgraded part well below H20/H200 performance. Total orders from Chinese customers exceed 6 million units, against a global 2027 production ceiling Nvidia has told partners is around 3 million units; sources say partners should expect only 5-10% fill rates. This is a cleaner demand-exceeds-supply signal than frontier-chip scarcity because it's happening at a part Nvidia specifically built to be less capable — real AI compute appetite is outrunning supply at every tier Nvidia is legally allowed to sell into China. Logged as new prediction P038 (75%: fill rate stays below 25% through 2027) since no existing prediction tests China-compliant-SKU allocation. Weakest link: anonymous trade sourcing, not a company disclosure; order books can be inflated ahead of expected rationing. NVDA -0.50% premarket despite the news, dragged down by broad sector weakness (see memory item below). (Investing.com/Reuters; Benzinga)

bull Amazon launches TorchNeuron — the first direct software-layer challenge to NVIDIA's CUDA moat this cycle

Amazon and Meta's PyTorch team unveiled TorchNeuron (Apache 2.0) at PyTorch Conference North America: a native PyTorch backend for AWS Trainium2/3 that runs existing PyTorch code with a single .to('neuron') device change — no rewrite required — supporting eager mode, torch.compile, FSDP2, tensor/pipeline parallelism, and FlashAttention. Built by Annapurna Labs (Amazon's chip-design unit); more than 30 early customers were already using it before today's public beta. Every prior custom-silicon entry this cycle (Maia 300, TPU, Trainium hardware itself) was a hardware announcement; this is the first move that directly targets the software lock-in — portability of existing PyTorch code — that has helped make NVIDIA the default choice regardless of raw chip economics. Doesn't move P003 (training-compute-specific); 30 early customers is not yet evidence of material workload migration. Worth watching for adoption numbers on Amazon's next earnings call. (TechCrunch; aiweekly.co)

Samsung, SK Hynix, Micron, and SanDisk have shed a combined >$700B of market value since October 6.
