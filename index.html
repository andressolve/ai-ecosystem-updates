watch SK Hynix conditions new US factory on relaxed chip-equipment export approval to its China fab

Reuters (exclusive, sources) reports SK Hynix is seeking Washington's approval to export more US chipmaking equipment to its existing Dalian, China facility — specifically to activate an unused section ("Mod B") for additional NAND production amid the ongoing memory shortage — as a condition attached to building a new US factory. Commerce Department policy to date has allowed existing China fabs to keep operating but has not approved equipment for capacity expansion or technology upgrades; this is the tension SK Hynix is negotiating.
