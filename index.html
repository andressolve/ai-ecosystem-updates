Overnight Developments

bear Broadcom: Google reportedly cut 2027 TPU orders ~20%, shifting to MediaTek

Ming-Chi Kuo of TF International Securities published a supply-chain survey (Friday, 11/20) estimating Google cut 2027 shipments of its Broadcom-designed TPU v8i ("Sunfish") series by roughly 20% (about 1 million units), with the volume shifting to MediaTek's competing v8t design. Neither Google nor Broadcom has commented or changed guidance. AVGO fell about 12% Friday (worst day since June 4) in a broad chip-sector selloff, so the analyst report can't be cleanly isolated from the index-wide move, but coverage attributes meaningful idiosyncratic underperformance to it. This is the first quantified, dated instance of the MediaTek-diversification risk already tracked in the Broadcom file (the "COT" risk) actually moving volume, rather than remaining a roadmap debate. It says nothing about total TPU demand or about custom silicon's standing versus NVIDIA — Google reallocating between two custom-silicon vendors is a Broadcom-specific share question, not a compute-demand one. Logged to watchlist/tier1/avgo.md and theses/computational-abundance.md. TF International note, via 24/7 Wall St.

watch NVIDIA: Alice Huang reporting deepens, Bessent's deadline lands this week

The New York Times and ABC News (11/22-23) reported that US intelligence tracked contacts between Alice Huang — the Megaspeed executive at the center of the export-control allegations — and China's Ministry of State Security during the period of her relationship with Jensen Huang. This adds specificity to the MSS-recruitment allegation already known since late October; it is not a new allegation. NVIDIA has said Megaspeed received only ordinary business information, no trade secrets, and nothing relevant to national security. No source reviewed shows NVIDIA itself had knowledge of any intelligence activity. Separately, premarket headlines recirculating Trump's comment that he "would not mind" Huang's return, and framing it as the cause of today's rally, are not new reporting — that comment and board chair Harvey Jones's refusal to reinstate Huang both date to the November 15-17 window already logged. Treasury Secretary Bessent's self-set 10-day deadline (from a November 15 Fox News interview) for the board and its outside counsel to answer export-control compliance questions runs out around November 25, this week. No prediction probability change — P042 holds at 65%. NYT · Bessent deadline, Al Jazeera

neutral A circulating "AI proved the Riemann Hypothesis" headline is a mischaracterization

Headlines this weekend ("Meta AI Chief Says Superintelligence Is Arriving Following AI Proof of Riemann Hypothesis") and a disputing mathematician's response both trace back to an Anthropic research post from August 2026, not a new result. That post never claimed a proof of the Riemann Hypothesis: it reported an unconditional improvement to a zero-density bound (the share of zeros known to lie on the critical line rose from 41.6% to 67.2%), reviewed internally and by two outside mathematicians (Brian Conrey, Dan Goldston), with a Lean-formalized component. The mathematician named in this weekend's dispute headline does not appear to be connected to Anthropic's actual work. This is a hype-cycle distortion of old, more modest evidence, not a new scientific-AI data point — worth flagging precisely because the computational-abundance thesis is supposed to be tested by evidence like this, and overstated claims are the pattern the thesis review is built to catch. No file changes; nothing here moves any tracker.
