Overnight Developments

[bear/watch] Texas expands its data-center permitting pause to a second agency

Governor Abbott ordered the Texas Commission on Environmental Quality (TCEQ) to halt all state-issued data-center-related permits until ERCOT completes its audit of the existing grid-interconnection waitlist (9/21). This is distinct from — and escalates — the statewide ERCOT/PUCT pause on new data-center grid interconnections already in place since August 3: that order froze new connections to the grid; this one freezes environmental permitting through a second state agency. BloombergNEF estimates almost 20% of the US 253GW data-center pipeline, or close to 50GW, is exposed to the Texas halt. This is an audit-driven pause, not a permanent ban, and doesn't affect capacity already interconnected or under construction — but it's the most concrete quantification yet of how much pipeline sits in the state with the single largest concentration of new AI data-center announcements. No prediction probability change; logged as an escalation to the existing power-constraint tracker in theses/computational-abundance.md.

[bear/watch] First sell-side bear case built on falling GPU rental prices

Rothschild Redburn initiated coverage on CoreWeave (Sell, $54 PT, ~34% downside) and Nebius (Sell, $84 PT, ~62% downside) on 9/21, citing three converging pressures: falling GPU rental prices, hyperscalers scaling captive fleets, and rising financing costs on multi-billion-dollar buildouts. The firm also flagged roughly $3T in aggregate off-balance-sheet AI-infrastructure commitments across the sector. This is a genuinely new argument for this framework's neocloud bear case — every prior CRWV/NBIS disconfirming entry this cycle centered on leverage covenants (CoreWeave's 8/3 term-loan repricing) or disclosure-quality gaps (Nebius's Vineland stop-work orders), not falling rental pricing. It also cuts against CoreWeave's own July SKU price increases (~25%, disclosed on its Q2 print) — worth checking actual GPU rental indices (SemiAnalysis, Silicon Data SDH100RT) against this claim rather than taking the sell-side assertion at face value. Separately, and unconnected to the Redburn call, a Blue Owl-sponsored, CoreWeave-leased 76MW Virginia data center began a ~$1.1B junk-bond sale the same day (Goldman-led, low-mid 9% yields, pricing expected 9/23) — a continuation of the SPV-financing pattern already tracked under sub-thesis #5, not a new mechanism. Logged to watchlist/tier2/coreweave.md and nebius.md.

watch Antitrust class action targets the four labs behind the "pace the frontier" call

A proposed class action was filed 9/18 in the Northern District of California (widely reported 9/20-21) against Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, and xAI, alleging the companies illegally colluded to slow AI development following Dario Amodei's September 12 essay (backed within hours by Sam Altman, Elon Musk, and Demis Hassabis). Four paid-subscriber plaintiffs allege the companies' agreement to pace capability improvement left customers paying unchanged subscription prices for slower advances, and are seeking treble damages, an injunction, and nationwide class certification. This bears directly on P037 (no lab/hyperscaler discloses an actual compute/capex cut attributable to "pace the frontier," held 92%) but doesn't test it: the suit alleges the rhetoric itself was a restraint of trade, not that any company has actually reduced compute spending — no probability change. Worth watching only if discovery in the case surfaces evidence of a real coordinated capacity reduction, which would bear on the prediction's 8% failure case.

Micron's second Taiwan union mediation also fails

A second mediation session between Micron and its Taoyuan-based unions (9/21) ended without a deal, extending the dispute first logged 9/7. The unions — representing over 80% of Micron's roughly 15,000 Taiwan employees — continue to seek a bonus equivalent to roughly 83 months of salary for FY26 plus a permanent structure allocating 15% of global operating profit to workers; the company has not presented a counter-proposal per union statements. Two consecutive failed mediations raise the probability this proceeds to an actual strike vote rather than resolving quietly. No impact to P015 (95%)/P028 (55%)/P029 (53%) — same supply-side execution-risk read as 9/7, a real (if still unrealized) risk to Micron's own Taiwan-heavy DRAM output layered on an already-undersupplied market. Logged to watchlist/tier2/micron.md.
