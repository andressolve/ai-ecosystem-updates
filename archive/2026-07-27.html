A weekend WSJ report (anonymously sourced; secondary outlets cite figures ranging $250B-$500B) says NVIDIA is providing a financing guarantee/backstop tied to OpenAI/SoftBank's ~10GW Ohio "Stargate"-adjacent buildout. Per source-hierarchy discipline, this stays a watch item, not evidence, until a company confirms terms. Logged in watchlist/tier1/nvda.md and theses/computational-abundance.md.

bull Brent falls ~8% on the first genuine multi-day de-escalation signal of the cycle — P008 upgraded 63% → 68% ↑

Brent dropped to ~$90.28-90.43 (from Thursday's ~$97-98 Red Sea-escalation level) after the US quietly paused strikes on Iran starting late Friday, Iran reciprocated by halting retaliatory strikes, and Tehran is reportedly in talks with Oman on Hormuz navigation. Unlike this cycle's many single-session V-shaped reversals, this is a multi-day, two-sided, diplomatically-corroborated pause. One unconfirmed residual incident (an IRGC-linked report of a tanker striking a naval mine in Hormuz over the weekend) hasn't moved price and isn't treated as fact. Upgraded modestly (not further) because the pause is quiet/unannounced, not a formal deal, and this cycle has produced de-escalation-then-relapse patterns before. MSFT/AMZN capex prints (7/29-30) remain the separate, decisive read on the demand side of P008.

neutral CXMT's STAR Market debut resolves as inconclusive — P029 held 53%

CXMT (ChangXin Memory) debuted on Shanghai's STAR Market 7/26-27 (~$4.4B raised, reportedly pricing above Samsung's market cap, posting a large first-day pop variously reported around +460%) — this was the pre-flagged checkpoint for whether Thursday's Korea memory-complex selloff (SK Hynix -8.34%, Samsung -7.59%) was rotation or a genuine competitive re-rating. Read as inconclusive, not a clean signal: the STAR Market has no daily price-limit for a new listing's first five sessions, so a large first-day pop is a float/mechanics artifact, not evidence CXMT has reached HBM3/volume-DRAM competitive scale. The genuine competitive questions (does CXMT reach volume scale, does it pressure ex-China ASPs) remain unresolved by a listing-day pop. No probability change; MU's own forward multiple and the SK Hynix/Samsung capacity-expansion plans remain the operative bear inputs.
