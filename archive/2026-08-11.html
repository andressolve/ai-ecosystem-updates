Overnight Developments

watch NVIDIA's $500B AI compute financing platforms

NVIDIA announced non-binding MOUs with six of the largest global asset managers/banks — Apollo, BlackRock, Blackstone, Brookfield, Goldman Sachs, KKR — to establish "AI Compute Infrastructure Financing Platforms" aiming to mobilize over $500B of third-party capital for AI infrastructure buildout across NVIDIA's ecosystem (frontier labs, enterprises, AI clouds). NVIDIA's framing: its compute is "an investable asset" with "the lowest token cost, highest revenue, and longest life." No named customers, no counterparty allocation, no disclosed NVIDIA guarantee obligation — these are MOUs, not definitive agreements. Source: NVIDIA Newsroom/IR (primary, 8/10); Blackstone press release (corroborating).

This is the largest, most institutionally formal instance yet of the vendor-financing pattern this repo has tracked since June (AVGO's AI XPU Platform, the FT's Compute SPV story). Structurally it's a lighter-risk version than Broadcom's model below — NVIDIA is positioning as originator/arranger, not guarantor. Logged to the thesis Supporting tracker as two-sided (sub-thesis #5).

bear BofA quantifies Broadcom's XPU-platform tail risk for the first time

BofA's credit desk downgraded Broadcom's issuer/bond rating Overweight → Marketweight, citing the Apollo+Blackstone AI XPU Platform (BofA calls the SPV itself "XPV"). New detail: Broadcom provides a residual value guarantee on most of the platform's debt — if a lessee (e.g., Anthropic) defaults and chip-resale proceeds fall short, Broadcom compensates senior tranche investors for the shortfall. On the initial $35B/1GW tranche, BofA models max exposure of $26B by Sept 2027 (max loss $2.9B) — consistent with the 8/4 FT Compute SPV story's $30B figure for that same initial tranche. Scaled to the full 20GW build-out by mid-2029, BofA models max exposure of $370B, max loss of $42B at 100% default / $10.5B at a 25% default assumption. AVGO bonds still priced only 30-45bp wider than non-XPU A-rated semi peers — a modest spread, not a distress signal. Source: BofA credit research note via wire (8/11).

Don't conflate the $30B (initial-tranche, near-term) and $370B (fully-scaled, 2029 projection) figures — different scope and time horizon. The mechanism matters more than the number: Broadcom is underwriting resale value on its own chips, a contingent liability that scales directly with platform size. Full detail and cross-reference to the 6/3 disclosure logged in watchlist/tier1/avgo.md.

Anthropic's compute-contracting spree continues: $9.1B, 20-year deal with Riot Platforms

Anthropic signed a $9.1B, 20-year cloud deal with Riot Platforms (Bitcoin miner turned AI data-center operator) for 191MW at Riot's Rockdale, TX campus, running through 2048 with extension options worth up to $16.1B total. Riot shares +25% after hours. This is Anthropic's third major compute deal in three months (SpaceX ~$45B in May, Volta $10B in July, now Riot $9.1B) — over $60B in contracted compute since May. Source: Bloomberg (8/11, primary reporting), corroborated by CoinDesk/The Block.

Continued-magnitude confirmation of Anthropic's now-established pattern of diversifying compute supply beyond hyperscalers into thin-equity/converted-industrial capacity — not a new category of evidence, so no thesis-file edit today, but worth flagging as the pace (three deals, three different counterparty types, 90 days) keeps accelerating.

Microsoft plans to unveil Maia 300 chip in September

The Information reports Microsoft is targeting a public unveiling of its next-gen Maia 300 AI accelerator "as soon as September," with talks underway to secure TSMC manufacturing capacity for 300,000+ units for 2027 delivery. Maia was introduced in Nov 2023 but has lagged peers (Google TPU, Amazon Trainium, Meta MTIA) in scaling. Source: The Information (8/10), corroborated by Reuters-sourced wire pickups.

Sub-thesis #4 (custom silicon as co-equal pillar) relevant if it materializes at the disclosed scale — but this is a schedule/plan report, not a shipped product or customer commitment. No thesis-file edit; watch for the September unveil itself as the next checkpoint.
