Overnight Developments

bear Anthropic's Google-TPU buildout financed through a ~$200B multi-guarantor "Compute SPV" web (FT)

The Financial Times reports Anthropic's compute buildout on Google TPUs is financed through special-purpose vehicles that purchase infrastructure using private-credit debt (Apollo, Blackstone) structured by Morgan Stanley, then lease the capacity to Anthropic — which is not itself investment-grade. Google guarantees the arrangement: an $815M current liability on its books, with disclosed exposure that could reach $44B in a tail/contingent scenario. Broadcom guarantees $30B of a $35B tranche, on top of its own already-disclosed $128B in AI-semis purchase commitments ($55.2B FY27 / $72.9B FY28). Crypto miners TeraWulf, Cipher, and Hut 8 supply data-center capacity (2.4GW across 10 projects, plus a separate 1.4GW/5-project/$15B tranche) toward a stated 4.5GW total planned TPU deployment. The most independently interesting detail: financing clears at a median ~7.1% rate when Google-backed vs. ~9.3% for otherwise-similar non-hyperscaler-backed neocloud capacity — direct, verifiable evidence that credit markets are already discriminating by guarantor quality rather than funding all AI capacity uniformly. Logged as Strong under sub-thesis #5 in theses/computational-abundance.md — the clearest primary-sourced picture yet of the Boeing/GE-style off-balance-sheet financing pattern this thesis has been tracking. Caveat: Broadcom's $128B figure is a purchase commitment, not revenue, and should not be conflated with its own AI-semis revenue trajectory (P020/P024); Google's $44B is contingent/tail exposure, not a current balance-sheet liability.

bull Palantir Q2 2026: US Commercial +149% YoY, guidance raised again

Palantir reported Q2 revenue of $1.94B (+93% YoY), with US Commercial revenue $764M, +149% YoY. Net income $1.06B ($0.41 diluted EPS vs. ~$0.35 est.). Management raised FY26 revenue guidance to $8.15-8.16B (from $7.65-7.66B, set just three weeks ago on 7/13) and raised the US Commercial FY26 target to >$3.42B (134% growth). Adjusted operating income guided to $4.889-4.897B; adjusted FCF to $4.5-4.7B. This is the strongest single-quarter application-layer signal logged this cycle that enterprise AI deployment is converting to durable production revenue rather than staying pilot-stage — see watchlist/tier2/pltr.md.

watch Anthropic signs $10B compute deal with thinly-capitalized neocloud Volta Infra

Anthropic committed to a 6-year, $10B compute contract with Volta Infra Holdings — a months-old, NVIDIA/Dell/a16z/Altimeter-backed startup with only ~$300M in disclosed equity and ~$5B in financing lined up against a $2.4B valuation (a ~33x ratio of contracted revenue to disclosed equity, structurally similar to CoreWeave's own early playbook). Separately, Bloomberg reports AI data-center developers are increasingly relying on bank letters of credit to substitute for their own thin credit when satisfying utility interconnection requirements, with Morgan Stanley signaling a high underwriting bar and some LCs reportedly issued before a lease or project financing was even in place. Both logged as Moderate under sub-thesis #5 — smaller-scale, less-disclosed versions of the same credit-substitution pattern as the FT story above.
