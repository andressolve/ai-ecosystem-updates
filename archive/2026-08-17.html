watch NVIDIA confirms SB Energy Ohio deal; aggregate financing-guarantee scope still unconfirmed

NVIDIA's Newsroom (primary, 8/17) confirmed a $1.5B direct investment in SB Energy, an exclusivity commitment on SB Energy's future US power-generation capacity, and credit support for an initial 4.25 IT-GW phase of a joint data-center buildout — first site "PORTS-Pike," Ohio. This is a concrete, named transaction inside the vendor-financing platform this repo has tracked since the 8/10 $500B MOU announcement and the 8/11-8/12 Huang 25%-per-project residual-value-guarantee clarification.

Separately, WSJ reported NVIDIA's aggregate financing-backstop exposure across this and related deals at under $120B — described as a scope-down from an earlier unconfirmed $250B WSJ figure (7/27). Neither number appears in NVIDIA's own release or any filing. Two different unreconciled press estimates for the same undisclosed structure in three weeks is a data-quality flag, not resolution — treat the dollar figure as unconfirmed pending an NVIDIA 10-Q/8-K. Logged two-sided to the thesis file and nvda.md; sharpens the still-open question of how NVIDIA's guarantee exposure compares to BofA's $370B/$42B full-scale Broadcom model.
