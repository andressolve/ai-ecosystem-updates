bull Applied Materials beats and raises; stock falls anyway

Applied Materials' FQ3 2026 report (8/13 AMC) beat on revenue and EPS and guided FQ4 above Street, citing strengthening leading-edge logic/foundry and HBM-related DRAM equipment demand. The stock traded down in the immediate aftermath. AMAT sits one layer upstream of TSMC/Samsung/SK Hynix in the equipment chain and isn't a memory vendor, so a beat-and-raise here is independent corroboration that tooling demand is still accelerating, not plateauing — consistent with Samsung's 7/30 guidance that the chip shortage persists through 2028. The stock reaction is the now-familiar world-vs-market pattern (AVGO 6/3, Samsung 7/7): a beat gets sold on valuation/positioning, not because the underlying number was bad. AMAT is not on the tracked watchlist (Tier 1/2/3) — flagged as a coverage gap worth a look given its role as an independent equipment-spend cross-check.
