Overnight Developments

watch DOJ opens formal antitrust investigation into NVIDIA's $20B Groq deal

The Justice Department opened an investigation into NVIDIA's December 2025 licensing deal with Groq shortly after it was announced, and has now sent NVIDIA a formal request for information (NYT, Reuters, Bloomberg — 9/9 evening through 9/10 morning). The deal (a "non-exclusive license" for Groq's inference IP plus the hire of ~90% of Groq's staff, including founder Jonathan Ross) was structured to avoid triggering Hart-Scott-Rodino merger review that a formal acquisition would have required. Reporting cites the likeliest outcome as a fine rather than a forced unwind. This is the first confirmation of an active probe with a document request — previously this risk existed only as a February letter from Senators Warren, Wyden, and Blumenthal urging FTC/DOJ scrutiny. No financial impact disclosed; this is a regulatory-structure risk to a playbook NVIDIA shares with Google (Character.AI) and Microsoft (Inflection), not a threat to NVIDIA's near-term inference roadmap. Filed to watchlist/tier3/groq.md and the thesis Disconfirming Evidence Tracker.
