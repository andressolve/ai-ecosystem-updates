Overnight Developments

bear Broadcom's CEO personally brokers a $63B Anthropic chip-financing deal — with Broadcom guaranteeing its own senior debt

WSJ reported (exclusive, 10/26 evening) that Broadcom CEO Hock Tan personally intervened to finalize $63B in financing — arranged by Bank of America, Citigroup, and Morgan Stanley (~$43.5B senior/first-lien, 69% of the total) plus a committed ~$19.5B second-lien tranche from Blackstone — for a newly formed special-purpose vehicle that will buy Broadcom AI chips and networking gear and lease them to Anthropic, with deliveries beginning 2027. Reuters and Bloomberg confirmed the structure. Broadcom itself is providing "residual value support" — a guarantee matching the senior tranche — meaning Broadcom is backstopping the same lenders financing purchases of its own hardware. DA Davidson upgraded AVGO on the news, framing Anthropic's S-1 as proof the AI cycle isn't over. AVGO +3.53% premarket.

This formalizes and enlarges the $60B ($42B senior/$18B Blackstone junior) structure this file has tracked since 10/2 into a named $63B deal — and for the first time, Broadcom is explicitly guaranteeing the senior tranche rather than just arranging or co-lending. Broadcom is now simultaneously chip vendor, financing arranger, and credit guarantor on the same transaction. The market's read (DA Davidson's upgrade) is bullish — capital keeps flowing at scale to the highest-conviction buildout. The bear read, unchanged in direction but sharper in degree: Broadcom's balance-sheet exposure to Anthropic credit risk now scales directly with how much hardware Anthropic buys. No prediction impact — P020/P024 resolve on revenue/order confirmation, not financing-structure risk. Reuters, Benzinga.

watch NVIDIA confirms $3B Meta equity commitment, deepens Muse partnership

NVIDIA and Meta announced (10/26 evening, NVIDIA Newsroom) that NVIDIA will invest $3B in Meta's equity offering — the largest of the three chipmakers' (NVIDIA/AMD/Broadcom) non-binding interest logged yesterday — while expanding their partnership on Meta's Muse agent and model family. Meta will deploy NVIDIA Vera Rubin, Vera CPUs, Spectrum-X Ethernet, and BlueField-4 to support Muse's agent infrastructure, with the two companies also collaborating directly to improve Muse model training/inference performance. Zuckerberg: "Muse is how we bring personal superintelligence to everyone."

This firms up yesterday's "non-binding interest" framing into a confirmed, sized, strategically-linked commitment — sharper than a pure equity purchase because NVIDIA's own infrastructure and model-layer involvement deepens at the same time. It's a cleaner circular-financing instance than last week's Samsung Electro-Mechanics item: the chip vendor is buying equity in the customer whose capex buys the vendor's chips, while also co-developing the product that will run on them. No prediction impact. NVIDIA Newsroom, Reuters.

bull TSMC reportedly weighing 10-20% CoWoS/advanced-packaging price hike

Trade press (DigiTimes-sourced, widely syndicated 10/26-27; TSMC has not commented) reports TSMC is considering a 10-20% price increase on CoWoS advanced-packaging services, citing AI GPU/ASIC demand straining packaging capacity, longer chip test times, and equipment-delivery lead times stretching from 3-6 months to 6-8 months. Would flow through to NVIDIA and AMD as customers.

New physical-layer pricing-power evidence consistent with the pattern already logged on TSMC's wafer pricing (9/24) and record Q3 print (10/15): demand for AI silicon is outstripping even a foundry that's actively tripling CoWoS capacity (>130K wafers/month in 2026 guided to 200K in 2027). Unconfirmed by TSMC directly. No prediction impact (P010/P011/P031 are already near-ceiling on revenue/margin, not packaging-price-specific). TipRanks, Investing.com.

neutral Microsoft offers buyouts to ~60,000 employees ahead of Wednesday's earnings

Microsoft is offering voluntary buyouts to roughly 60,000 employees — more than a quarter of its ~223,000-person global workforce as of its own June 30, 2026 disclosure — covering the US, Canada, India, and UK, limited to staff scoring 70 or below on Microsoft's internal "experience" scale (reaching up to VP level). Severance scales with level (one week of pay per six months tenure at ≤64, two weeks at 65-67) plus 6-12 months of stock vesting and limited healthcare continuation. The acceptance window runs to early December, with departures following that month.

The largest disclosed Microsoft workforce action of this AI cycle, reported as reshaping the cost base to help fund continued AI capex — a cost-reallocation-for-AI data point, not a demand signal, landing one day ahead of Wednesday's FY2027 Q1 earnings. No prediction impact (P013/P014 test cloud revenue/capex levels, not headcount). CNBC, TechCrunch.
