Overnight Developments

bull CoreWeave/NVIDIA: first production Vera Rubin NVL72 deployment, 4.8x throughput claim

At its Fully Connected event in San Francisco, CoreWeave announced it has put NVIDIA's Vera Rubin NVL72 racks into production, with Cognition (maker of the Devin coding agent) as the first customer running production workloads. Cognition's own benchmarks: up to 4.8x total token throughput per GPU vs. a GB200 NVL72 baseline on SWE-2 agentic-inference workloads at matched interactivity, and 3.8x output token throughput per GPU on reinforcement-learning workloads. New Spectrum-X 102.4T Ethernet networking was also disclosed. This is self-reported, single-workload-type data with no disclosed model size, context length, or batch settings — an early-access showcase, not an independent benchmark — but it's the first production (not lab or paper) performance read on the generation that follows Blackwell/GB200, and it lands at CoreWeave rather than a hyperscaler captive fleet. CoreWeave · NVIDIA · StorageReview

watch Musk confirms TSMC-Terafab talks; Intel falls, TSMC hits a record

Tim Culpan's Culpium newsletter reported (10/2) that TSMC is exploring ways to help operate the planned fabs for Musk's Texas "Terafab" chip venture (linked to Tesla, SpaceX, and xAI); Musk confirmed talks are underway (10/3) but said only that "something may come of" them — no ownership structure, capital commitment, or site/timeline has been disclosed. Reported possible models: TSMC owns and operates a new fab while Musk-orbit companies provide capital or long-term volume commitments, or a joint venture where TSMC contributes process technology and operational expertise. Intel fell ~4% on the read-through (a potential loss of Terafab exclusivity, since Intel had been the only other named partner in Musk's chip-manufacturing plans). TSMC's Taiwan listing (2330.TW) closed +3.00% at a fresh record the same session — likely a combination of the Terafab headline, a strong Hon Hai (Foxconn) sales beat reported the same morning, and the broader AI-complex rally, rather than attributable to any single cause. TSMC enters its pre-earnings quiet period today (10/5) ahead of the 10/15 Q3 call — no further company commentary expected until then regardless of how the Terafab story develops. Culpium · MarketWatch · TrendForce

bear Amazon discloses $68B in blocked/delayed data centers, responds with $1B community fund

Amazon disclosed that roughly $68B of data-center projects were delayed or blocked in Q2 2026 alone, across ~100 proposed local bans or restrictions nationally — the largest quantified figure yet for community/permitting opposition as a binding constraint on hyperscaler buildout pace. In response, AWS unveiled "Built Together," a five-year, $1B community-investment program (workforce pathways, energy/water affordability support, flexible local funding) and committed to stop requiring NDAs with government agencies during data-center site negotiations. This is a distinct constraint from the chip-supply and power-generation bottlenecks this framework usually tracks: local political opposition doesn't respond to capex or lead time the way a fab or turbine order does. It cuts both ways — a genuine, quantified drag on buildout pace, but also the clearest signal yet that a company is willing to spend real money (not just PR) to clear it, which only makes sense if underlying demand remains intact. Tom's Hardware · GeekWire
