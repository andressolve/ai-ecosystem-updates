Overnight Developments

bull SK Hynix posts first-ever ₩100T+ quarter; reaffirms shortage through 2030

SK Hynix's Q3 2026 results (company release, 10/28 Seoul): revenue ₩104.62T (~$78.9B, +328% YoY, +32% QoQ), operating profit ₩83.33T (80% operating margin, +632% YoY), net profit ₩39.78T (38% net margin), gross margin 86%. DRAM was 75% of revenue, NAND 24%. Management said HBM4 will overtake HBM3E in shipment and revenue share in Q4, is "accelerating M15X ramp-up to reach full capacity by end of 2026," and reiterated that demand will outpace supply "through 2030" given leading-edge process complexity and fab construction lead times. The board separately approved a ₩20T treasury-share buyback (part of a full-year 2026 shareholder-return plan of ~₩100T/$72B: at least ₩30T in cash dividends, at least ₩60T in buybacks/cancellations) and declared a ₩20,000/share Q3 dividend (₩10,000 regular + ₩10,000 special, tied to Kioxia-investment gains). Stock closed Seoul +10.89% to ₩1,487,000, outperforming the Kospi.

This is the single highest-quality, most direct confirmation of the memory-undersupply thesis logged to date — an 80% operating margin from a commodity-adjacent supplier is a number TSMC and NVIDIA themselves don't print, and "through 2030" extends management's own horizon on the supply/demand gap beyond anything previously stated on the record. Cross-reference: P015 (DRAM undersupply persists through C3Q26) is already at its 95% ceiling with no room to move further on this evidence — logged as reinforcing, not probability-changing. SK hynix 3Q26 results, Investing.com, CNBC.

watch OpenAI reportedly plans to buy Google TPUs; Google opens TPU reselling to Oracle

The Information reported (10/27 evening, single-sourced, no financial terms disclosed) that OpenAI plans to buy Google TPUs — a notable development given OpenAI has historically been NVIDIA's largest customer and is also building a custom chip with Broadcom for deployment by end-2026. Separately, and more concretely, Google confirmed (Alphabet announcement, 10/28) that Oracle will become the first cloud platform besides Google Cloud to offer TPU services, launching in 2027 — opening Google's custom-silicon business beyond its own captive infrastructure for the first time since TPUs launched in 2013.

The Oracle deal is the harder data point (named partner, confirmed launch timing) and extends the merchant-TPU-channel pattern this file has tracked since the Google-Blockstone BXN1 vehicle (5/18): TPU now has captive-Cloud, BXN1-standalone, and Oracle-resale distribution simultaneously. The OpenAI report, if accurate, would add a fourth major lab (after Anthropic, Meta, and the Trainium-committed hyperscalers) exploring non-NVIDIA silicon, but it's unconfirmed, doesn't disclose training-vs-inference split or volume, and OpenAI already runs substantial Trainium capacity — this doesn't move P003 (which resolves on an explicit lab statement about training-compute majority, and centers on Anthropic). Logged to the sub-thesis #4 (custom silicon co-equal pillar) supporting tracker as context, no probability change. The Information via Reuters, CNBC.
