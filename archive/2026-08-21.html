Overnight Developments

watch Broadcom reportedly in talks for $60-100B AI debt financing covering Anthropic and OpenAI

Bloomberg (8/20, sourced to people familiar with the talks) reports Broadcom is negotiating with a lender group to raise more than $60B — potentially up to $100B in aggregate — to fund custom-chip infrastructure for Anthropic and OpenAI. Reported structure: a ~$30B junior debt tranche plus a Broadcom guarantee on a $60-70B senior-secured tranche, with Apollo Global Management and Blackstone (the same partners from the 5/8-5/11 $35B mandate) in talks to participate. Not finalized; no term sheet confirmed. This is the next visible tranche of the financing architecture tracked since May and now closer to BofA's full-scale $370B/$42B-max-loss model (8/11) than the original $35B tranche was. Two-sided: a deal this size confirms real, scaling demand (bull), but the same Broadcom-guaranteed recourse structure keeps growing (bear, sub-thesis #5). P020 (92%) and P024 (95%, OpenAI Phase 1-specific) both held — this reported deal is broader in scope and unconfirmed, not a resolution of either. Logged to theses/computational-abundance.md, watchlist/tier1/avgo.md, and a P024 context note in predictions/prediction-log.md.

This is the most direct on-record supply/demand-gap quantification from a memory vendor this cycle — logged Strong supporting (sub-thesis #1/#3) to theses/computational-abundance.md.
