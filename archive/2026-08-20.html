Overnight Developments

watch Google takes Marvell warrants tied to ~$120B of attach-silicon purchases

Marvell's SEC 8-K (primary) discloses Google holds warrants for 58,970,907 Marvell shares at a $206.58 strike, vesting in $500M-purchase tranches of Marvell "ATTACH" silicon — full vesting implies ~$120B in cumulative purchases over an undisclosed multi-year horizon. This is adjacent/attach silicon, not the TPU compute die itself, which remains Broadcom's franchise. MRVL fell -7.82% to $216.00 on the pre-8-K report (8/18); AVGO fell same-day on the read-through. The $120B is a warrant-vesting ceiling tied to an incentive structure, not a signed revenue contract — Google earns equity upside by buying, it isn't obligated to. This directly confirms watchlist/tier1/avgo.md's own 6/3/26 flag that Google was "looking elsewhere" for diversification; no AVGO prediction changes (P020 92%, P024 95% both test Broadcom's own AI semi revenue). Next test: Marvell's Q2 FY2027 earnings 8/27.
